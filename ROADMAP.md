# Fabrica Runtime Roadmap

## F0 — Seed
Product identity, provenance, canonical working tree.

## F1 — Fabricated
Installable fabrica-runtime distribution, `import fabrica as fab`, working smoke tests, CI, community files.

## F2 — Operational
Verified release, stable examples, first Fabrica-native capability primitive.

## F3 — Integrated
Adapters for other Fabrica products and shared portfolio conventions.

## F4 — Native
Canonical capability IR, graph runtime, explicit effect/authority/provenance semantics.

## F5 — Sovereign
Inherited substrate becomes replaceable; Fabrica semantics remain authoritative.
